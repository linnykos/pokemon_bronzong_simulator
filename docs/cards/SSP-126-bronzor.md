## Notes for the simulator

A near-twin of [Bronzor PRE 66](PRE-066-bronzor.md): also **Metal**, also
retreat 1, also **Buddy-Buddy Poffin-eligible**. The only meaningful difference
is **60 HP instead of 70**.

### PRE 66 vs SSP 126

Both are Poffin-eligible and both carry the same downside of being Metal (see
the PRE 66 file). Choosing between them is close to arbitrary for the turn-1/2
metric, since neither is attacking and neither survives being attacked in a way
the metric measures. **PRE 66's extra 10 HP is a free tiebreaker** — 70 HP is
still under the Poffin cap, so it costs nothing.

The only reason to prefer SSP 126 would be an effect keyed to low HP; nothing in
these decklists is.

All three evolve into Bronzong TEF 69, and a list may run a **mix** — up to 4
Bronzor total across printings, since they share the name. A split list (e.g. 2
TEF 68 + 2 PRE 66) keeps both the Poffin targets and the Telepathic targets, and
is worth testing in part 6 as its own candidate.
