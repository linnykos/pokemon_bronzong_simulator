# Dusknoir

- **Set / number:** Prismatic Evolutions (PRE) #37
- **Regulation mark:** H
- **Category:** Pokémon — **Stage 2**, evolves from Dusclops
- **Type:** Psychic
- **HP:** 160

## Ability

**Cursed Blast**
> Once during your turn, you may put 13 damage counters on 1 of your opponent's
> Pokémon. If you use this Ability, this Pokémon is Knocked Out.

## Attacks

**Shadow Bind** — `[P][P][C]` — 150 damage
> During your opponent's next turn, the Defending Pokémon can't retreat.

## Other

- **Weakness:** Darkness x2 | **Resistance:** Fighting -30 | **Retreat cost:** 3

## Notes for the simulator

- 4 copies. Reached via **Rare Candy** from Duskull (2 Dusclops is too few to
  be the main route).
- Rare Candy carries the same evolution timing restrictions: not on the
  player's first turn, and not onto a Duskull benched that same turn.
- Competes with Bronzong for the turn-2 play. The decision tree must decide
  which the player prioritizes when both are available.
