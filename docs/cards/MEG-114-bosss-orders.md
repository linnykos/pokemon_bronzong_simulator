# Boss's Orders

- **Set / number:** Mega Evolution (MEG) #114
- **Regulation mark:** I
- **Category:** Trainer — **Supporter**

## Effect

> Switch 1 of your opponent's Benched Pokémon with their Active Pokémon.

## Notes for the simulator

- Targets the **opponent's** board only. It does **nothing** for getting our
  own Bronzong Active, and it consumes the Supporter slot that Hilda or
  Lillie's Determination wants.
- Effectively dead weight for the turn-2 target event. A prime candidate for
  cutting when optimizing consistency in part 6 — with the caveat that the
  simulator does not measure games won, only turn-2 Evolution Jammer rate, so
  this card's real value is invisible to the metric. Flag this in results.
- 2 copies.
