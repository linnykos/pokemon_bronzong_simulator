# Special Red Card

- **Set / number:** Chaos Rising (CRI) #82
- **Regulation mark:** J
- **Category:** Trainer — **Item** (not an ACE SPEC)
- **Appears in:** decklist3 (1).

## Effect

> You can use this card only if your opponent has 3 or fewer Prize cards
> remaining. Your opponent shuffles their hand and puts it on the bottom of
> their deck. If they put any cards on the bottom of their deck in this way,
> they draw 3 cards.

## Notes for the simulator

- **Strictly unplayable in the window this project measures.** It requires the
  opponent to be at **3 or fewer Prizes**, and on turns 1-2 both players still
  have all 6.
- For the simulator it is a **dead card** in the opening: it occupies a deck
  slot, can be discarded freely to Ultra Ball, and should be the first thing the
  discard policy pitches.
- decklist3's clearest candidate cut for turn-1/turn-2 consistency — again, the
  metric cannot see its late-game value.
