# Telepathic Psychic Energy

- **Set / number:** Perfect Order (POR) #88
- **Regulation mark:** J
- **Category:** **Special Energy**
- **Appears in:** decklist2 (4), decklist3 (4). **Not** in decklist1.

## Effect

> As long as this card is attached to a Pokémon, it provides `[P]` Energy.
> When you attach this card from your hand to a `[P]` Pokémon, search your deck
> for up to 2 Basic `[P]` Pokémon and put them onto your Bench. Then, shuffle
> your deck.

## Notes for the simulator

- **It provides `[P]`, so it does pay Evolution Jammer's cost** — unlike
  Enriching Energy, which provides `[C]` only.
- **The attach trigger is enormous for this deck.** Attaching it to a `[P]`
  Pokémon searches out **up to 2 Basic `[P]` Pokémon straight onto the Bench**.
  In decklists 2 and 3 the Basic `[P]` Pokémon are **Bronzor**, **Duskull**, and
  **Latias ex** — so one attachment can find Bronzor *and* Latias ex, which is
  most of the setup this deck needs.
- The search puts them on the **Bench**, never into the Active spot. So it
  solves "I have no Bronzor" but not "my Bronzor isn't Active".
- Bronzor and Bronzong are both `[P]`, so the trigger is live whether the Energy
  goes onto Bronzor on turn 1 or onto Bronzong later.
- **It is a Special Energy**, so unlike basic Energy it is capped at **4** —
  decklists 2 and 3 run the maximum. It is also **not recoverable with Night
  Stretcher**, which retrieves only *Basic* Energy.
- **Decklists 2 and 3 contain no basic Energy at all.** Their only `[P]` sources
  are these 4 copies, and Enriching Energy cannot substitute. That makes their
  Energy base more fragile than decklist1's 4 basic Psychic, and the
  prize-and-draw sensitivity of these 4 cards is a headline measurement for
  part 6.
- Hilda searches for "an Energy card", which includes Special Energy, so Hilda
  can still fetch it.
