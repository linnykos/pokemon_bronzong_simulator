# Poké Pad

- **Set / number:** Perfect Order (POR) #81
- **Regulation mark:** J
- **Category:** Trainer — **Item**

## Effect

> Search your deck for a Pokémon that doesn't have a Rule Box, reveal it, and
> put it into your hand. Then, shuffle your deck.
> (Pokémon ex, Pokémon V, etc. have Rule Boxes.)

## Notes for the simulator

- **Free search with no discard cost**, unlike Ultra Ball — but restricted to
  Pokémon **without** a Rule Box.
- In decklist1 the legal targets are: **Bronzor, Bronzong**, Duskull, Dusclops,
  Dusknoir, Buneary, Flutter Mane, Budew.
  It **cannot** find Mega Lopunny ex, Mega Kangaskhan ex, Meowth ex, or
  **Latias ex**.
- So Poké Pad is an excellent Bronzong-line enabler and a non-answer for the
  Latias ex free-retreat plan. That asymmetry matters for the decision tree:
  Ultra Ball is the only Item that can find Latias ex.
- An **Item**: shut off in the `item_lock` scenario.
- 4 copies.
