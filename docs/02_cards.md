# Card Index

Verbatim card text lives one file per card in `docs/cards/` — **47 files
covering 45 distinct cards**. Two pairs are alternate printings folded together
by `canonical_card_id()`: MEE 5 / SVE 5 (basic Psychic Energy) and TEF 144 /
MEG 167 (Buddy-Buddy Poffin). This page is the index and the cross-decklist
summary, and the matrix below is **generated** by
`scripts/refresh_card_index_claude.R`, which also checks coverage in both
directions — every database row has a file, and every file has a row.

Text is transcribed from limitlesstcg.com, and cards whose details drive the
decision tree are queried a **second time with a pointed question**, against a
different source. That is not a formality: the first pass mis-rendered two
attacks as Abilities (Evolution Jammer and Itchy Pollen), and **Dunsparce JTG
120's Trading Places is the third** — Limitless lists it as an Ability, while
pokemon.com and Bulbapedia both give it as an attack costing `[C]`. As an
Ability it would have been a free rung on the §4.3 ladder; as an attack it is
Run Around's shape and a last resort. Anything still unconfirmed is marked
`[UNVERIFIED]` in its file and listed under "Open items" below.

All eight decklists in `decklists/` are legal 60-card lists. **decklist7 and
decklist8 are a different deck**, not a variation on the shell the first six
share: they drop Mega Kangaskhan ex, Boss's Orders and Ciphermaniac's, cut Rare
Candy to 1, and add a Darkness sub-theme (Munkidori, basic Darkness Energy) plus
Gwynn, Risky Ruins and Buddy-Buddy Poffin. Read their rows against each other
rather than against the first six.

### The Bronzor decision

| Printing | Type | HP | Poffin-eligible? | Findable by Telepathic Psychic Energy? |
|----------|------|----|------------------|----------------------------------------|
| [TEF 68](cards/TEF-068-bronzor.md) | Psychic | 80 | **no** | **yes** |
| [PBL 63](cards/PBL-063-bronzor.md) | Metal | 80 | **no** | **no** |
| [PRE 66](cards/PRE-066-bronzor.md) | Metal | 70 | **yes** | no |
| [SSP 126](cards/SSP-126-bronzor.md) | Metal | 60 | **yes** | no |

**PBL 63 is the printing that fetches worst**, and it is the one decklist7 and
decklist8 run: 80 HP puts it over Poffin's cap *and* being Metal keeps it off
Telepathic's list, so it is reachable only by Poké Pad, Ultra Ball and Brock's
Scouting. Paired with TEF 68, also 80 HP, the consequence is blunt — **Buddy-Buddy
Poffin cannot fetch a Bronzor at all in either of those lists**, and its two
copies there can only reach Duskull, Buneary and Dunsparce, none of which
advances sub-goal A.

All three are named "Bronzor" and all three evolve into Bronzong TEF 69, so a
list may run any **mix** up to 4 total. The trade is clean and worth measuring
directly in part 6: Poffin access versus Telepathic Psychic Energy access. A
split (e.g. 2 TEF 68 + 2 PRE 66) keeps both and is its own candidate. Of the two
Metal printings, **PRE 66 is the free pick** — 70 HP is still under the Poffin
cap, so its extra 10 HP costs nothing.

## What this transcription pass established

1. **Evolution Jammer is an attack costing `[P]`, 30 damage** — not an Ability.
   Bronzong must be **Active** with a **Psychic** Energy attached.
2. **Bronzong has no Ability**, which makes it a legal **Salvatore** target.
   Dusclops and Dusknoir both have the Cursed Blast Ability and so can *never*
   be fetched with Salvatore.
3. **Salvatore works on the player's first turn** (confirmed with the player,
   2026-08-29) — it overrides both the played-this-turn and the first-turn
   evolution restrictions. Going **second**, a Salvatore list can therefore
   attack with Evolution Jammer on **turn 1**, locking the opponent's evolutions
   on their own first turn. Going first it cannot (no Supporter, no attack), so
   the earliest achievable turn depends on the *(decklist, coin flip)* pair —
   see `docs/01_rules_standard.md` §5.1.
4. **Enriching Energy provides `[C]`, not `[P]`** — it cannot pay for Evolution
   Jammer. **Telepathic Psychic Energy provides `[P]` and can.**
5. **Telepathic Psychic Energy's attach trigger** searches up to 2 Basic `[P]`
   Pokémon onto the **Bench** — in these lists, Bronzor, Duskull, and Latias ex.
   It fixes "no Bronzor", not "Bronzor isn't Active".
6. **Latias ex's Skyliner reads "Your Basic Pokémon in play have no Retreat
   Cost."** Passive, works from the Bench, live from setup. The cheapest way to
   get Bronzor Active on turn 1 — but it is a **1-of**, and **Poké Pad cannot
   find it** (Rule Box). Only Ultra Ball can.
7. **Buneary's Run Around** switches into Bronzor for free, but it is an
   **attack**, so it exists only when going **second**.
8. **Mega Kangaskhan ex is a Basic** — hence no Kangaskhan in any list. Run
   Errand draws 2 but requires it to be **Active**, competing with Bronzor.
9. **Mega Lopunny ex is a Stage 1**, so Rare Candy serves **Dusknoir only**.
10. **Budew's Itchy Pollen is an attack**, so the player going *first* can never
    use it on turn 1. Item lock only exists against an opponent who went second.
11. **Special Red Card is unplayable on turns 1-2** (needs the opponent at ≤3
    Prizes) — a dead card in the measured window and the first thing to pitch to
    Ultra Ball.
