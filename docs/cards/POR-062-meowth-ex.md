# Meowth ex

- **Set / number:** Perfect Order (POR) #62
- **Regulation mark:** J
- **Category:** Pokémon — **Basic**
- **Type:** Colorless
- **HP:** 170

## Ability

**Last-Ditch Catch**
> Once during your turn, when you play this Pokémon from your hand onto your
> Bench, you may use this Ability. Search your deck for a Supporter card,
> reveal it, and put it into your hand. Then, shuffle your deck.
> You can't use more than 1 Ability that has "Last-Ditch" in its name each turn.

## Attacks

**Tuck Tail** — `[C][C][C]` — 60 damage
> Put this Pokémon and all attached cards into your hand.

## Other

- **Weakness:** Fighting x2 | **Resistance:** none | **Retreat cost:** 1

## Notes for the simulator

- Ability triggers **only when played from hand onto the Bench** — so it does
  **not** trigger from a setup placement, and it does **not** trigger if Meowth
  ex is placed as the Active. It must be benched from hand during a turn.
- Turn-1 tutor for any Supporter — in practice Hilda (fetches Bronzong + an
  Energy) or Lillie's Determination.
- **Going first, this is much weaker:** it finds a Supporter, but the first
  player cannot play a Supporter on turn 1. The found card is still useful for
  turn 2. Going second it is a full turn-1 Supporter chain.
- Only **1 copy**; prizeable.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** Prize cards given up. Assumed 2 (standard `ex`); irrelevant
  to the target event.
