# Dusclops

- **Set / number:** Prismatic Evolutions (PRE) #36
- **Regulation mark:** H
- **Category:** Pokémon — **Stage 1**, evolves from Duskull
- **Type:** Psychic
- **HP:** 90

## Ability

**Cursed Blast**
> Once during your turn, you may put 5 damage counters on 1 of your opponent's
> Pokémon. If you use this Ability, this Pokémon is Knocked Out.

## Attacks

**Will-O-Wisp** — `[P][P]` — 50 damage

## Other

- **Weakness:** Darkness x2 | **Resistance:** Fighting -30 | **Retreat cost:** 2

## Notes for the simulator

- Only 2 copies in decklist1 against 4 Dusknoir — the line is built to be
  reached with **Rare Candy**, not by evolving through Dusclops.
- Abilities work the turn a Pokémon evolves, so Cursed Blast is live
  immediately. Irrelevant to the turn-2 Evolution Jammer target, but it is a
  competing use of the turn-2 evolution and of Rare Candy.
