# Basic Psychic Energy

- **Set / number:** Scarlet & Violet Energies (SVE) #5; also printed as Mega
  Evolution Energy (MEE) #5 — **the same card**, see
  [MEE-005](MEE-005-psychic-energy.md)
- **Regulation mark:** none (basic Energy; always legal)
- **Category:** **Basic Energy**

## Effect

> Provides `[P]` Energy.

## Notes for the simulator

- **The only card in decklist1 that can pay Evolution Jammer's `[P]` cost.**
  Enriching Energy provides `[C]` and does not qualify.
- Appears as `Basic {P} Energy SVE 5` in decklist1 (4 copies) and as
  `Psychic Energy MEE 5` in decklist5 (2 copies). Same card — the simulator must
  treat the two printings as one.
- With 4 copies and 6 prizes, the chance that *all* copies are prized is
  negligible, but the chance of drawing none in the opening window is not — and
  there is no Energy search in these decks other than **Hilda**, which fetches
  an Evolution Pokémon and an Energy together.
- Note that decklist5 pairs 2 of these with 4 Telepathic Psychic Energy, giving
  it **6** `[P]` sources — the most of any list, and the only list that can
  recover a `[P]` source with Night Stretcher.
- The Energy may be attached on **turn 1 to the Bronzor** (it carries through
  evolution) or on **turn 2 to the Bronzong**. Attaching on turn 1 is strictly
  better when the player already knows Bronzor will be the attacker, because it
  frees the turn-2 attachment for Enriching Energy's draw 4.
- Basic Energy has **no 4-copy limit** — the count is a free parameter for
  part 6 to optimize.

**Verified:** standard basic Energy card.
