# Munkidori

- **Set / number:** Twilight Masquerade (TWM) #95
- **Regulation mark:** H
- **Category:** Pokémon — **Basic**
- **Type:** Psychic
- **HP:** 110

## Ability

**Adrena-Brain**
> Once during your turn, if this Pokémon has any `[D]` Energy attached, you may
> move up to 3 damage counters from 1 of your Pokémon to 1 of your opponent's
> Pokémon.

## Attacks

**Mind Bend** — `[P][C]` — 60 damage
> Your opponent's Active Pokémon is now Confused.

## Other

- **Weakness:** Darkness x2 | **Resistance:** Fighting -30 | **Retreat cost:** 1

## Notes for the simulator

- **Adrena-Brain is inert for this metric.** It moves damage counters, and no
  damage inside the measured window changes whether Evolution Jammer can be used;
  no opponent's board is modelled to move them to either.
- **It is not inert for the searches, though.** It is a Basic `[P]` Pokémon, so
  **Telepathic Psychic Energy can fetch it onto the Bench** and it is a legal
  want-list filler. At 110 HP it is **over Buddy-Buddy Poffin's 70 HP cap.**
- Having an Ability makes it ineligible as a Salvatore target, which is moot: it
  is a Basic and Salvatore fetches Evolutions.
