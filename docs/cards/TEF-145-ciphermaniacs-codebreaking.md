# Ciphermaniac's Codebreaking

- **Set / number:** Temporal Forces (TEF) #145
- **Regulation mark:** H
- **Category:** Trainer — **Supporter**

## Effect

> Search your deck for 2 cards, shuffle your deck, then put those cards on top
> of it in any order.

## Notes for the simulator

- Does **not** put cards in hand — it stacks the top of the deck. The player
  draws 1 per turn, so a turn-1 Codebreaking delivers exactly **1** of the two
  chosen cards on turn 2 (unless a draw effect gets the second).
- Because it is a search, it **cannot find prized cards** — and importantly, it
  is the cleanest way for the player to *learn* a card is prized. The decision
  logic should treat a failed search as information.
- Supporter: unavailable turn 1 going first, and it competes with Lillie's
  Determination and Hilda for the once-per-turn Supporter slot.
- 3 copies.
