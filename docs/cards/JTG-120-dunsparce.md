## Notes for the simulator

- **Trading Places is an ATTACK, not an Ability**, and the distinction is the
  whole card. As an Ability it would be a free switch and a new cheap rung on the
  §4.3 ladder; as an attack it costs `[C]` — the turn's one Energy attachment —
  and **ends the turn**, which makes it structurally identical to Buneary's Run
  Around and subject to every caveat in §4.2: last resort only, never on turn 2,
  and never paid with the only `[P]` source in hand.
- **This is the third time the transcription source has mis-rendered an attack as
  an Ability** in this project, after Evolution Jammer and Itchy Pollen. Limitless
  lists Trading Places under "Ability"; pokemon.com and Bulbapedia both list it as
  an attack costing one Colorless. The two independent sources are what the entry
  above follows.
- At 70 HP it is a legal **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** target, which matters in
  decklist8 where no Bronzor is — both Bronzor printings there are 80 HP.
- Modelled with no Ability. Trading Places is **specified and not implemented**;
  see the note in `docs/03a_card_playbook.md`.
