# Blissey ex

- **Set / number:** Twilight Masquerade (TWM) #134
- **Regulation mark:** H
- **Category:** Pokémon — **Stage 1**, evolves from **Chansey**
- **Type:** Colorless
- **HP:** 300
- **Rule Box:** yes (Pokémon ex)

## Ability

**Happy Switch**
> Once during your turn, you may move a Basic Energy from 1 of your Pokémon to
> another of your Pokémon.

## Attacks

**Return** — `[C][C][C]` — 180 damage
> You may draw cards until you have 6 cards in your hand.

## Other

- **Weakness:** Fighting x2 | **Resistance:** none | **Retreat cost:** 4

## Notes for the simulator

- **It evolves from Chansey, and neither decklist7 nor decklist8 runs a
  Chansey.** There is therefore no legal way to put it into play in either list:
  Rare Candy goes Basic → Stage 2 and cannot reach a Stage 1, and Salvatore
  fetches a card that evolves from a Pokémon *we control*. **The card is dead in
  both lists as written** — this is a decklist question for Kevin, not a
  simulator one.
- Were a Chansey added, Happy Switch would still not move a **Telepathic Psychic
  Energy**, which is a Special Energy rather than a Basic one. It would move a
  basic Psychic Energy, and neither list runs one.
- Modelled as a Pokémon with an Ability and a Rule Box, and otherwise inert.
