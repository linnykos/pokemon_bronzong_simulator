# Buneary

- **Set / number:** Phantasmal Flames (PFL) #83
- **Regulation mark:** I
- **Category:** Pokémon — **Basic**
- **Type:** Colorless
- **HP:** 70

## Attacks

**Run Around** — `[C]` — no damage
> Switch this Pokémon with 1 of your Benched Pokémon.

**Kick** — `[C][C]` — 20 damage

## Other

- **Weakness:** Fighting x2 | **Resistance:** none | **Retreat cost:** 1

## Notes for the simulator

- **Run Around is a switching effect that costs an attack, not the turn's
  retreat.** Going second, a turn-1 Buneary Active with any Energy attached can
  attack Run Around to put Bronzor into the Active spot for free — without
  spending Switch and without needing Latias ex. Going first this is
  unavailable, since the first player cannot attack.
- This makes Buneary a materially better turn-1 lead when going second than
  when going first, and the decision tree must branch on it.
