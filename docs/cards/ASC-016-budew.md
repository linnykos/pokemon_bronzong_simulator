# Budew

- **Set / number:** Ascended Heroes (ASC) #16
- **Regulation mark:** H **[UNVERIFIED — see note]**
- **Category:** Pokémon — **Basic**
- **Type:** Grass
- **HP:** 30

## Attacks

**Itchy Pollen** — no Energy cost — 10 damage
> During your opponent's next turn, they can't play any Item cards from their
> hand.

*(Budew has no Ability. Itchy Pollen is an **attack** with a zero Energy cost —
confirmed on a targeted second query, because the first transcription
incorrectly rendered it as an Ability.)*

## Other

- **Weakness:** Fire x2 | **Resistance:** none | **Retreat cost:** 0

## Notes for the simulator

- **This is the `item_lock` scenario card, on both sides of the table.**
- As the *opponent's* card: if they go first — no, they cannot, the first
  player cannot attack. Itchy Pollen can therefore only be used by the player
  going **second**, on their turn 1. It then locks our Items during **our
  turn 2** — exactly the turn we need Rare Candy, Ultra Ball, Switch, and Poké
  Pad. This is the disruption scenario worth modelling.
- As *our* card: same logic in reverse. Note that using it means Budew is
  Active on our turn 1, so we need a switch to get Bronzor Active for turn 2.
- Retreat cost 0 — free to retreat away regardless of Latias ex.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** Regulation mark. The source reported `H` for this card and
  for Night Stretcher ASC #196, but `I` for Nighttime Mine ASC #197 — cards in
  one set share a mark, so at least one is wrong, and `H` looks like it was
  carried over from the original Shrouded Fable printings. Every candidate
  (H/I/J) is Standard-legal, so this does not affect legality or the
  simulation.
