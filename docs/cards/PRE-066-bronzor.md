## Notes for the simulator

**This is a different card from [Bronzor TEF 68](TEF-068-bronzor.md)**, not a
reprint. Both are named "Bronzor", so **both evolve into Bronzong TEF 69** —
evolution matches on name, not on type or printing.

### Why you would run it — Buddy-Buddy Poffin

At **70 HP** it is fetchable with [Buddy-Buddy
Poffin](TEF-144-buddy-buddy-poffin.md) ("70 HP or less"). Bronzor TEF 68, at 80
HP, is **not**. That single point is the entire argument for the swap: it turns
Poffin into a free Item that puts Bronzor on the Bench, live on turn 1 even when
going first.

### What you give up

- **It is Metal, not Psychic.** [Telepathic Psychic Energy](POR-088-telepathic-psychic-energy.md)
  searches for *Basic `[P]` Pokémon* — a Metal Bronzor **cannot be found by it**.
  And attaching that Energy to a Metal Bronzor does **not** trigger its search,
  since the trigger requires a `[P]` Pokémon. (Attaching to the evolved
  **Bronzong**, which is Psychic, still triggers it.)
- It no longer counts toward [Mystery Garden](MEG-122-mystery-garden.md)'s
  `[P]`-Pokémon tally.
- Different Weakness (Fire rather than Darkness) — negligible for this metric.

### Retreat cost

Retreat 1 rather than TEF 68's 3. **Note this matters less than it appears:**
retreating costs the Energy of the Pokémon *leaving* the Active spot, so
Bronzor's own retreat cost is irrelevant to *promoting* it. It matters only when
Bronzor or Bronzong is Active and wants out — which is not part of the target
line.
