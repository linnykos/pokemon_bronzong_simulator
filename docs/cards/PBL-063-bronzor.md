## Notes for the simulator

- **The fourth distinct card named "Bronzor", and the one that fetches worst.**
  At 80 HP it is over Buddy-Buddy Poffin's 70 HP cap, and being **Metal** it is
  not a legal Telepathic Psychic Energy search target either. So it is reachable
  only by Poké Pad, Ultra Ball and Brock's Scouting — the opposite trade-off from
  PRE 66 and SSP 126, which are Poffin-findable but not Telepathic-findable.
- Consequence for decklist7 and decklist8, which run it alongside Bronzor TEF 68
  (also 80 HP): **Buddy-Buddy Poffin cannot fetch any Bronzor in those lists at
  all.** Its only legal targets there are Duskull, Buneary and Dunsparce.
- Attaching a Telepathic Psychic Energy to it still pays sub-goal D and simply
  fires no search, exactly as for the other Metal printings.
