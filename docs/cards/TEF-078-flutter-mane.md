# Flutter Mane

- **Set / number:** Temporal Forces (TEF) #78
- **Regulation mark:** H
- **Category:** Pokémon — **Basic**
- **Type:** Psychic
- **HP:** 90

## Ability

**Midnight Fluttering**
> As long as this Pokémon is in the Active Spot, your opponent's Active Pokémon
> has no Abilities, except for Midnight Fluttering.

## Attacks

**Hex Hurl** — `[C][C][C]` — 90 damage
> Put 2 damage counters on your opponent's Benched Pokémon in any way you like.

## Other

- **Weakness:** Metal x2 | **Resistance:** none | **Retreat cost:** 1

## Notes for the simulator

- Ability requires Flutter Mane to be **Active**, which conflicts with getting
  Bronzor/Bronzong Active. It is an answer to opposing Ability-based Actives,
  not part of the Evolution Jammer line.
- Retreat 1, free under Skyliner, so leading with it costs little if Latias ex
  is down.
- 1 copy; a reasonable candidate to cut when optimizing for turn-2 consistency.
