# Bronzong

- **Set / number:** Temporal Forces (TEF) #69
- **Regulation mark:** H — **Standard 2026 legal**
- **Category:** Pokémon
- **Stage:** Stage 1 — evolves from Bronzor
- **Type:** Psychic
- **HP:** 110

## Attacks

**Evolution Jammer** — `[P]` — 30 damage
> During your opponent's next turn, they can't play any Pokémon from their hand to evolve their Pokémon.

**Super Psy Bolt** — `[P][C][C]` — 100 damage

## Other

- **Weakness:** Darkness x2
- **Resistance:** Fighting -30
- **Retreat cost:** `[C][C][C]` (3)

## Notes for the simulator

- This is the **target card** of the whole project. Evolution Jammer is an
  **attack**, not an Ability, so Bronzong must be **Active** with **1 Psychic
  Energy** attached, on the player's own **turn 2** at the earliest.
- The lock also stops **Rare Candy**, since the Stage 2 would be played from
  the opponent's hand to evolve.
- Retreat cost 3 does **not** obstruct the target line. Retreating costs the
  retreat cost of the Pokémon *leaving* the Active spot, so getting Bronzor
  Active is priced by whatever is currently Active — typically a Basic, and
  therefore **free** whenever Latias ex's Skyliner is in play. Bronzong's own 3
  matters only after it is Active and wants out.
