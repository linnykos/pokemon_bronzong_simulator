# Dusclops

- **Set / number:** Shrouded Fable (SFA) #19
- **Regulation mark:** H
- **Category:** Pokémon — **Stage 1**, evolves from Duskull
- **Type:** Psychic
- **HP:** 90

## Ability

**Cursed Blast**
> Once during your turn, you may put 5 damage counters on 1 of your opponent's
> Pokémon. If you use this Ability, this Pokémon is Knocked Out.

## Attacks

**Will-O-Wisp** — `[P][P]` — 50 damage

## Other

- **Weakness:** Darkness x2 | **Resistance:** Fighting -30 | **Retreat cost:** 2

## Notes for the simulator

- **Identical in every respect that matters to Dusclops PRE 36** — same Ability,
  same HP, same retreat, same stage and same evolves-from. The two are alternate
  printings of one card and the simulator treats them as separate rows only
  because the decklists cite different set codes.
- It is therefore a full participant in the §4.3 rung-5 **Cursed Blast escape**:
  the policy matches the Active by NAME, not by id, so an SFA 19 Dusclops opens
  the same door a PRE 36 one does.
