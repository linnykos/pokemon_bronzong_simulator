# Brock's Training

- **Set / number:** Hidden Fates (HIF) #55
- **Regulation mark:** **none**
- **Category:** Trainer — **Supporter**
- **Status:** ⚠️ **NOT Standard 2026 legal**, and not usable by this deck.

## Effect

> Attach an Energy card from your hand to 1 of your Geodude, Graveler, Golem,
> Onix-GX, Cubone, Rhyhorn, Rhydon, or Sudowoodo.

## Why this card cannot be used

Two independent reasons, either of which is fatal:

1. **It is not Standard-legal.** Hidden Fates is a 2019 Sun & Moon-era set. The
   card carries **no regulation mark**, and a name search on Limitless returns
   **only this one printing** — there is no reprint carrying H, I, or J. Under
   the reprint rule (`docs/01_rules_standard.md` §1), a card with no regulation
   mark is legal only if some printing of it *is*; none is.
2. **Its effect does nothing here.** It attaches Energy only to Geodude,
   Graveler, Golem, Onix-GX, Cubone, Rhyhorn, Rhydon, or Sudowoodo. **None of
   these appears in any of the six decklists**, so even in a format where it were
   legal it would be a blank card.

## Did you mean a different card?

Nothing named "Brock's Training" exists in a current set. If the intent was a
Standard-legal Supporter, the near-neighbours are:

- **Brock's Grit** — a real Supporter name, but likewise an older card; would
  need its own legality check.
- **[Surfer](SSP-187-surfer.md)** (SSP 187) — the switch-and-draw Supporter
  imported alongside this one, and the card that actually does something for
  this deck.
