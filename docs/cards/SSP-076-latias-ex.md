# Latias ex

- **Set / number:** Surging Sparks (SSP) #76
- **Regulation mark:** H
- **Category:** Pokémon — **Basic**
- **Type:** Psychic
- **HP:** 210

## Ability

**Skyliner**
> Your Basic Pokémon in play have no Retreat Cost.

## Attacks

**Eon Blade** — `[P][P][C]` — 200 damage
> During your next turn, this Pokémon can't attack.

## Other

- **Weakness:** Darkness x2 | **Resistance:** Fighting -30 | **Retreat cost:** 2

## Notes for the simulator

- **The single most important support card for the turn-2 Evolution Jammer
  line.** Skyliner is a passive Ability that works from the Bench and is live
  the moment Latias ex is in play — including from setup, on turn 1.
- Effect: any **Basic** in the Active spot can retreat for **free**. So on
  turn 1 the player can lead with any Basic, bench Bronzor and Latias ex, and
  retreat into Bronzor at no cost, preserving Switch for later.
- Note the restriction: it applies to **Basic** Pokémon only. Bronzong is a
  Stage 1 and does *not* get free retreat — irrelevant here, since we want
  Bronzong to stay Active.
- Only **1 copy** in decklist1, so it is prizeable ~10% of the time. The
  sensitivity of the turn-2 rate to this count is a headline result for part 6.
