# Night Stretcher

- **Set / number:** Ascended Heroes (ASC) #196
- **Regulation mark:** H **[UNVERIFIED — see Budew note on ASC marks]**
- **Category:** Trainer — **Item**

## Effect

> Put a Pokémon or a Basic Energy card from your discard pile into your hand.

## Notes for the simulator

- Recovery, not setup. On turns 1-2 the discard pile is only whatever Ultra
  Ball pitched, so this is mainly an Ultra Ball cost-mitigator: pitch a Bronzor
  to Ultra Ball, retrieve it with Night Stretcher.
- Note it retrieves **Basic** Energy only — it cannot get back Enriching Energy.
- 1 copy; low impact on the turn-2 metric. Candidate cut.
