# Mystery Garden

- **Set / number:** Mega Evolution (MEG) #122
- **Regulation mark:** I
- **Category:** Trainer — **Stadium**
- **In decklists:** none yet — imported as a **candidate**.

## Effect

> Once during each player's turn, that player may discard an Energy card from
> their hand in order to draw cards until they have as many cards in their hand
> as they have `[P]` Pokémon in play.

## Notes for the simulator

- A **refill, not a draw**: it tops the hand *up to* the number of `[P]` Pokémon
  in play. With a small board it does little; the deck must already be developed
  for it to pay.
- **It costs an Energy card from hand** — and in these lists `[P]` Energy is the
  scarcest resource (4 copies in most lists, and the only way to pay Evolution
  Jammer). Discarding one to draw is often directly counterproductive to the
  target event. Enriching Energy is the natural card to feed it.
- Counts `[P]` **Pokémon in play**, both Active and Benched. With Bronzor **TEF
  68** (Psychic) the count is easy to build; with the **Metal** Bronzor
  printings (PRE 66, SSP 126) those Bronzor no longer count toward it — another
  knock-on effect of the Bronzor choice.
- **Symmetrical** — the opponent may use it too, on their own turn.
- Occupies the once-per-turn Stadium slot, competing with Jamming Tower /
  Festival Grounds / Nighttime Mine.
