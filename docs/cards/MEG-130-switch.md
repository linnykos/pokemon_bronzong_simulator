# Switch

- **Set / number:** Mega Evolution (MEG) #130
- **Regulation mark:** I
- **Category:** Trainer — **Item**

## Effect

> Switch your Active Pokémon with 1 of your Benched Pokémon.

## Notes for the simulator

- **The primary answer to "Bronzor is on the Bench".** Does not use the turn's
  retreat, costs no Energy, and works regardless of retreat cost.
- Redundant with Latias ex's Skyliner when the Active is a **Basic** — but
  Skyliner does not help if the Active is already an Evolution, so Switch is
  the more general tool.
- An **Item**, so it dies to Itchy Pollen in the `item_lock` scenario.
- 4 copies. The interaction between the Switch count and the Latias ex count is
  a key thing for part 6 to measure — they partly substitute for each other.
