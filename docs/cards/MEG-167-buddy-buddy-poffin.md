# Buddy-Buddy Poffin

- **Set / number:** Mega Evolution (MEG) #167
- **Regulation mark:** H
- **Category:** Trainer — **Item**

## Effect

> Search your deck for up to 2 Basic Pokémon with 70 HP or less and put them onto
> your Bench. Then, shuffle your deck.

## Notes for the simulator

- **The same card as Buddy-Buddy Poffin TEF 144**, reprinted.
  `canonical_card_id()` folds this id onto `TEF-144`, exactly as it folds
  `MEE-005` onto `SVE-005`, so every rule in the simulator sees one card rather
  than two.
- The 70 HP cap is what makes the printing of Bronzor matter, and in decklist7
  and decklist8 it bites hard: **both Bronzor printings those lists run are 80
  HP** — TEF 68 and PBL 63 — so Poffin cannot fetch a Bronzor in either. Its
  legal targets there are Duskull (60), Buneary (70) and Dunsparce (70), none of
  which advances sub-goal A.
