# Enriching Energy

- **Set / number:** Surging Sparks (SSP) #191
- **Regulation mark:** H
- **Category:** **Special Energy**

## Effect

> As long as this card is attached to a Pokémon, it provides `[C]` Energy.
> When you attach this card from your hand to a Pokémon, draw 4 cards.

## Notes for the simulator

- **Critical caveat: it provides `[C]`, not `[P]`.** Evolution Jammer costs
  `[P]`, so **Enriching Energy alone cannot pay for Evolution Jammer.** The
  simulator must not treat it as a substitute for a basic Psychic Energy.
- Its real role in the window we care about is **drawing 4 cards** on the
  attachment — but that consumes the turn's one Energy attachment, which is the
  same resource the Psychic Energy needs. On turn 1 that trade is usually fine
  (draw 4 now, attach Psychic on turn 2); on turn 2 it is usually wrong.
- 1 copy; prizeable.
